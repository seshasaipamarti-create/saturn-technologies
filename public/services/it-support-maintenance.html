IT Support and Maintenance

Keep your website and software running smoothly after launch.

Overview

A website needs care after launch. We help with updates, fixes, content changes and performance, so small problems do not turn into big ones.

What this includes

  • Fixing bugs and broken functionality
  • Content and design updates
  • Technical assistance when something is not working
  • Reviews of speed and performance, with improvements

Good for

  • Businesses without an in-house technical person
  • Sites that were built by someone else and need ongoing care
  • Owners who want a dependable contact when something breaks

How we work on it

We begin with a requirement discussion, then send a written proposal covering scope, timeline and price. Once you approve it we design, build, test and launch, and we remain available for support afterwards.
