Custom Web Application Development

Software built around how your business actually works, from internal dashboards to customer portals.

Overview

When off-the-shelf software does not fit, we build web applications tailored to your process. We start by understanding how your team works today, then design software that removes manual effort instead of adding to it.

What this includes

  • Requirement discussion and a written scope before development starts
  • User accounts, roles and permissions where needed
  • Dashboards, forms, search and reporting designed for daily use
  • Integration with tools you already use, where an integration is available
  • Testing before launch and support after launch

Good for

  • Teams managing work in spreadsheets and messages
  • Businesses that take bookings or appointments
  • Companies tracking stock, orders or customers
  • Organisations that need a portal for customers or staff

How we work on it

We begin with a requirement discussion, then send a written proposal covering scope, timeline and price. Once you approve it we design, build, test and launch, and we remain available for support afterwards.
