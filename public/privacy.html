1. Who we are

This website is operated by Saturn Technologies India Private Limited ("Saturn Technologies", "we", "us"). This policy explains how we handle personal information that you give us through this website. You can contact us through the form on our Contact page.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you send an enquiry through our contact form we collect the details you enter: your name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in, your project description, and, if you choose to provide them, your company name and estimated budget.

Technical information

Our website is delivered through Vercel. Like most web hosting services, Vercel may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested and the time of the request, in order to deliver the website, keep it secure and prevent abuse. We use your IP address in a limited way to protect our contact form from spam, by counting how many submissions arrive in a short period.

3. Why we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss your requirements.
  • To prepare proposals and, where you become a customer, to provide our services.
  • To keep this website and our contact form secure and to prevent spam and abuse.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies and does not currently use analytics or tracking tools. If we add analytics or any non-essential cookies in future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

5. How your information is stored and who receives it

When you submit the contact form, the details are sent by email to our business mailbox. This website does not keep a database of enquiries. Your enquiry is therefore held in our email system and in any records we keep to manage your enquiry or project.

To run the website and deliver your enquiry we use the following third-party services, which may process information on our behalf:

ServicePurpose
VercelHosting this website and running the contact form
ResendDelivering contact form submissions to us by email

We may also share information with professional advisers, such as accountants and lawyers, and with authorities where the law requires it. Some of these providers may process information on servers outside India.

6. How long we keep information

We keep enquiry details only for as long as we need them to respond to you and to manage any resulting relationship, and for as long as the law requires us to keep business and tax records. When information is no longer needed we delete it or anonymise it.

7. Security

The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The contact form is validated on our server, protected against automated submissions and limited in how often it can be used. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your privacy requests

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you may withdraw any consent you have given. To make a request, contact us through the form on our Contact page. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request, and we may keep information that we are legally required to keep.

9. Children

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Links to other websites

This website may link to other websites, including social media pages and demonstration projects. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with its "last updated" date.
