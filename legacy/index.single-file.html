FAQ

Questions we hear often

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and features. After a short call we send a fixed quote, so there are no surprise charges later.

Do you host the website for me?

Yes, if you want us to. We offer managed hosting with free SSL, daily backups and monitoring. It is optional, and you can move your site to any other host at any time.

How long will it take?

A typical business website takes 3 to 5 weeks once we have your content. Web apps and online stores take longer, and the proposal includes a dated plan.

Can I edit the website myself?

Yes. We set it up so you can change text, images and products without touching code, and we show you how during handover.

Do I own the website after it is built?

Yes. Once the final payment is made, the code, designs and accounts are yours.

Do you offer support after launch?

Every project includes 30 days of free fixes. After that you can choose a monthly maintenance plan or call us only when needed.
